I

The ground

A drawing of the machine that does not exist yet

The rover does not have a chassis. What has been driven is a skid-steer model in Gazebo, and the flight controller on a desk with nothing on the motor outputs. The drawing is here so the geometry has a body. The numbers beside it were measured on the model.

A 20 cm tag at 8 m is 11 pixels and was never seen. At 6 m it is 16 pixels and flickers. At 4.5 m and 3.0 m it is seen in every frame, and the range comes out about 3 percent long. That error is still unexplained.

II

The split

The Pi thinks. The board drives. Either one can die.

The Raspberry Pi runs the manager, the vision node, localization, and the executive. It speaks MAVLink. It does not own the motors.

The Pixhawk owns the motors, the failsafes, and the last heartbeat it heard. If the Pi goes quiet, the board goes to HOLD on its own clock. That was measured in SITL at 3.6 seconds.

Tools that talk to the board use system id 246. System id 245 is the manager. A tool that heartbeats as 245 and then exits latches the GCS failsafe even while the rover is disarmed.

III

A marker leg

GNSS gets it close. The camera finishes it.

The GNSS point is 6 m short of the post. Search needs three frames of the same tag. One or two frames are forgotten after 15 seconds. Three or more become a place on the ground, and a retry drives back to that place instead of starting the spiral again.

Inside 4.5 m the approach servos on the pixels. Outside that it aims a point short of the tag by the standoff minus the board's waypoint radius. It calls the leg done when the camera is within 1.75 m of the estimate and the tag was seen on this pass.

The first finish put the camera 1.67 m from the tag and then failed the verify, because the camera was pitched 8 degrees down and the tag had left the top of the frame. The model now pitches the camera 17 degrees up. The next headless run finished in 30 seconds, camera at 1.69 m, body 2.09 m from the post.

IV

The quiet Pi

The board does not wait to be told the link is dead

Link liveness is any message from the board inside 2 seconds, not a heartbeat alone. Mode and armed state still come from the last heartbeat. A guided velocity command expires after 3 seconds if nothing refreshes it.
